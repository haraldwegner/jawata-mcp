# v3.16.0 — the store reports its own drift, and an analogy carries its cause

Two findings from dogfooding the released build, both about knowledge that was
present and invisible.

## The store says which stories it does not have

`experience(kind=stats)` now walks its own file substrate and reports the files no
row cites: `unloadedFiles`, the offending names, and a warning that says the cure
rather than the fault.

The failure it exists to end, measured on 2026-08-27: four stories were authored,
cold-read, stamped, committed and reported as remembered — while the store held none
of them. The reseed that ingests a story lived only in instruction text, and it was
skipped. Every surface agreed everything was fine, because every surface asked the
store what it **had** rather than whether the files had **arrived**.

An absent substrate answers `UNKNOWN` and an unreadable one says so. Neither reports
a comforting zero: "nothing was found" and "nothing was looked for" are different
facts, and only the second is a fault.

This number is what the studio's stop gate reads to hold a turn that wrote a story
and never reseeded it. A store older than this release answers with no drift number,
and that hook fails open — deliberately, and visibly.

## An analogy carries its cause

`cause` — the Minto complication, first-class since v3.15.0 — reached the entry
renderer and the nomination candidates, and was missed on the third path: the
analogy carrier. A live recall returned five comparable experiences and not one of
them said what problem it solves.

The analogy needs it most. Its framing is *judge whether it transfers*, one symptom
has many causes, so a list of analogies **is** a differential — and without the
diagnoses it asks the reader to discriminate on a summary alone. Both the structured
answer and the rendered line now carry it, from one map, so the two cannot disagree.
An entry with no cause emits no key and no empty parenthetical: absence stays absence.

## Compatibility

No schema change — v15 stores are unaffected, and no migration runs. Both additions
are read-side.
